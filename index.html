From scattered chaos
to one calm screen.

Five real moments from a merchandising floor — and what changes the instant Excel AI OS takes over.

Desk 01 · Merchandising

"Where is PO #4362?"

Three buyers calling at once. Every answer lives in a different stack of paper, a different person's memory.

→ One dashboard shows every PO's live status — no calls needed.
Desk 02 · Follow-ups

Sticky notes as a memory system

Follow-ups tracked on paper get lost the moment the note falls off the monitor.

→ Automated reminders fire themselves — nothing depends on a sticky note surviving the day.
Desk 03 · Critical Follow-Ups

Five people, one whiteboard, no live status

PO confirmations, fabric approvals, lab dips, trims — all tracked by hand, all one missed update away from a delay.

→ Live order tracking — every stage, updated the moment it changes.
Desk 04 · The Full Floor

Dozens of people. Dozens of versions of the truth.

Every desk has its own numbers. None of them agree by Friday.

→ Integrated reports — one click, accurate, the same numbers for everyone.
Desk 05 · Today's Priorities

Urgent, on paper, easy to miss

Critical POs flagged in red marker — easy to miss if you didn't walk past the whiteboard today.

→ Real-time alerts on email and WhatsApp — the urgent things find you, instead of waiting to be noticed.
