FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Excel automation for a garment factory?

It connects orders, fabric, trims, production and dispatch data in one Excel-based system, so reports, alerts and costing update automatically instead of being typed by hand.

Do you build Power Apps?

Yes. We build Power Apps for shop-floor entry, approvals and KPI alerts on Android, synced live with SharePoint and Excel.

How does a QR code inventory system work?

Excel generates a unique QR code for each piece. Staff scan pieces one by one with a phone or scanner, and stock, stage and reports update on every scan.

Is our business data safe?

Our systems are built on Microsoft platforms, so data sits on Microsoft servers, encrypted in transit and at rest, with automated versioned backups and role-based access.

Which industries do you serve?

Garment manufacturers, furniture makers and interior designers, along with other make-to-order businesses that run on spreadsheets.
